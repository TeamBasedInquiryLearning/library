---
title: 2026 Version of TBIL Resource Library Released
date: 2026-07-28
author: Drew Lewis
---

The 2026 versions of our TBIL activity books (for Precalculus, Calculus, and Linear Algebra) are
now available in the [TBIL Resource Library](https://library.tbil.org).  
These feature updates, corrections, and additions contributed by members of the TBIL Community
over the past year.

We are also excited to announce that we are publishing a printed version of the 
_Linear Algebra for Team-Based Inquiry Learning_ activity book.  The official launch 
date is August 15, with preorders available now through 
[619 Wreath Publishing](https://www.619wreath.com/store/p56/Lin-alg-TBIL.html).  
The HTML version of the books will always remain freely available on this website in
the [TBIL Resource Library](https://library.tbil.org).
